# Rocket showcase V15 — pre-render review (not approved)

Status: proposed; do not dispatch a renderer until owner explicitly approves. V14 remains the homepage version. No existing media, CFD samples, or renderer has been modified.

## Verified V14 findings
- The current homepage references `assets/rocket-cfd-build-v14.mp4`. V14 construction is a Canny/silhouette edge mask revealed by a vertical wipe, not sequential CAD feature drawing.
- V14 seeds 1,400 upstream candidates plus 300 nose candidates and 420 wake/reverse candidates, then selects local nose and wake paths. Its inlet selection requires eventual exit and rejects projected body-core overlaps, which can bias against genuine slow or recirculating trajectories.
- V14 uses three-dimensional tetrahedral interpolation, uniform time scaling, a CAD occupancy volume, and a common pressure scale. Keep these foundations, but verify registration and replace the trajectory selection/lifetime logic.
- The original GLB nose centerline in the V14 display-coordinate depth is +0.2127 in (nominal radius 0.4375 in). CFD wall samples are near zero. A preliminary robust radial fit on the cylindrical axial interval (-2.7,-1.5) in, using zero-speed samples in a 0.37–0.51 in radial ring, gave approximate depth centers of +0.008, +0.012, +0.010 in for biconic, hemisphere, blunt. Median radial residuals before/after: 0.1684/0.0105, 0.1667/0.0139, 0.1662/0.0110 in. This is a diagnostic fit, not a complete surface/fin registration validation.

## Proposed changes for approval
1. Register original CAD to each CFD field. Validate body, nose, tail and fins separately; preserve axial scale and actual fin orientation. Use the identical transform for render, occupancy and depth buffer. Report residuals and surface coverage; do not silently apply an arbitrary 0.21 in translation.
2. Rebuild construction as CAD drafting: upright original geometry, thin datum/centerline, progressively drawn meaningful silhouette and feature curves (body, nose, fins), short complete wireframe hold, then shaded surfaces. Avoid exposing tessellation edges, vertical scan wipes, invented hardware, or arbitrary dimension annotations. Rotate smoothly into the exact horizontal CFD pose; maintain material and projection through the handoff. Use matching typography and caption layout with the other homepage films.
3. Seed particles only at the supported upstream inlet, including a stratified near-axis band; use an upstream-only warm-up to populate the domain. Preserve real 3D trajectories, including non-exiting paths. No direct nose/wake seeds, artificial attraction, velocity modification, time-based despawning, or collision-triggered resets. Recycle only at a genuine supported-domain exit. Distinguish visible crop exits from unsupported interpolation.
4. Use adaptive integration with swept-segment solid checks, including across integration substeps and interpolated display positions. Check against conservative registered CAD occupancy and independently audit near-wall tracks against triangle geometry. Reject unsafe steps, reduce step size, and report unresolved trajectories; never render penetration or silently fabricate wall-following motion. If a path cannot be validated, flag it as numerically unresolved, not stagnation. Do not claim exact physical accuracy from finite mesh data.
5. Make stagnation visible through uniformly slowed advection, adequate nose-region inlet sampling, readable bright heads and short time-based trails, and a longer stable flow shot. Do not artificially collect particles or pin them at walls. Reverse-flow trajectories must originate upstream; a steady isolated recirculation region may have no upstream-connected path. If unsupported, leave it unfilled rather than fabricate circulation. A separate explicitly labeled diagnostic velocity plot could be considered later if needed.
6. Use actual depth-buffer occlusion; fin overlap in screen projection is not a collision. Audit physical coordinates independent of visibility. Clearly distinguish center-plane static pressure colors from 3D projected tracers. Retain shared physical crop, source pressure values, common scale and uniform slowdown across biconic, hemisphere and blunt. No new CFD solve.
7. Proposed delivery: native high-resolution render (target at least 1920 px wide, preserve homepage aspect ratio), 30 fps H.264 MP4, yuv420p, faststart, separate matching WebP poster, V15 filenames. Proposed timing: 0–2.5 s CAD lines, 2.5–4.5 s surface reveal, 4.5–5.2 s completed hold, 5.2–7.2 s alignment, about 8 s per CFD case. Timing and resolution are proposals, not approved.
8. New workflow must use workflow_dispatch only, with no push trigger. Keep V14 unchanged. Validate before encoding and publish preview assets only; switch index.html only after owner reviews the output. Existing V14 and earlier workflows are push-triggered on their own listed script/data paths, so do not edit those paths during preparation.
