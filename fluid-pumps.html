01 / QUESTION

How does the pump design affect its role?

The investigation compares how pumps transfer energy to a fluid and how fluid direction, impeller design, and pressure versus flow requirements influence pump selection. It is a comparative research project, not a claim that I designed or experimentally tested a commercial pump.

02 / TECHNICAL BACKGROUND

Three ways to move fluid

Centrifugal: Fluid enters near the impeller center and moves radially outward. The casing or diffuser converts part of the fluid's velocity into pressure.

Axial-flow: The rotating blades primarily push fluid parallel to the shaft. This approach is commonly associated with relatively high volume flow at a lower pressure rise per stage.

Mixed-flow: Combines radial and axial components, occupying an intermediate range of flow and head characteristics.

03 / ENGINEERING TRADEOFFS

Compare designs in context

No single pump configuration is universally best. The appropriate choice depends on required flow rate, head, efficiency, fluid properties, system curve, and operating constraints. My ongoing work focuses on understanding and communicating those tradeoffs.
