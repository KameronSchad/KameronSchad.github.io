INDEPENDENT STUDY / FEB-MAY 2026

Confidential Client-Sponsored Mechanical Design

Served as the primary mechanical CAD contributor on a four-person multidisciplinary team developing a confidential client-sponsored prototype. Work included enclosure design, component integration, tolerances, and presentation, with client-specific functionality and proprietary details withheld.

01 / REQUIREMENTS

Design around components that already exist

Physical components and interfaces imposed packaging, attachment, clearance, and access requirements. Balanced space constraints, manufacturing tolerances, and assembly needs while maintaining confidentiality about the product's intended function.

02 / CAD AND MEASUREMENTS

Use the real parts instead of trusting nominal dimensions

Created most of the SolidWorks models for the four-part housing and measured eight physical components with calipers. Refined fit adjustments and press-fit features to ensure CAD geometry matched the hardware used in the assembly.

03 / CROSS-DISCIPLINARY DESIGN

Make room for other engineers' requirements

Collaborated with electrical and computer engineering teammates to translate component dimensions, mounting interfaces, and access needs into mechanical design requirements. Revised the housing to accommodate the electronics while preserving practical assembly.

04 / OUTCOME

Recognized as a team accomplishment

Presented the completed team project at a student showcase, receiving recognition among independent-study projects. Led most mechanical CAD work within the multidisciplinary effort; final integration and presentation reflected contributions from the full four-person team.

05 / SHARING AND LESSONS

Protect the client's work

Detailed client CAD models and technical drawings are withheld pending permission for public release. Transferable engineering work includes packaging, measurement, tolerance decisions, and multidisciplinary communication.

Confidentiality is part of engineering professionalism.
