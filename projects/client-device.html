01 / FIT AND PACKAGING

Use physical measurements, not just nominal dimensions

Created most of the SolidWorks housing models and measured eight actual components with calipers. Adjusted mounting features and press-fit allowances around the hardware being integrated.

Balanced packaging, access, attachment, and clearance requirements with the constraints of a compact assembly.

02 / CROSS-DISCIPLINARY WORK

Translate electronic interfaces into mechanical requirements

Worked with electrical and computer engineering teammates to account for component placement, connections, cable space, and assembly access.

Presented the completed independent-study project at a student showcase with the four-person team.

TAKEAWAY

Designing mechanical interfaces for a multidisciplinary prototype.

Primary responsibility: mechanical CAD and dimensional verification.

Presented the completed prototype and design poster at a public showcase; unpublished CAD remains private.
